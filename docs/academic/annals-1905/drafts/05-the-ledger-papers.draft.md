# DRAFT — THE LEDGER PAPERS

*[First draft, J. Fosse, June–July 1905. The draft BELIEVES the conjecture
the revision refutes, carries the 9,100× figure the erratum corrects, and
resolves the rungs only against the balance fold. Undrum's letter of 30
June ("write what does NOT survive with the same pen") and Skavlan's of 19
July are folded into the draft's own leaves. The revised memoir is Paper
V. — Ed.]*

**Johanne Fosse**

## §1

At season's end I close the volume: summary, seal, kept tail. The
question: what may be asked of the closed volume afterwards?

## §2. Folds

A summary rule combines entries by an operation associative and
commutative; the fold of a volume is independent of order (one entry more
at a time; swaps of neighbors reach every order). Undrum's two clerks is
this lemma in a clerk's clothing; Grønn's shop within a shop is its
shadow.

## §3. What survives

Every question covered by the fold is answerable from the closing. And —
this draft's claim — **conversely, nothing of consequence is lost**: the
questions the trade asks are balances, counts, and totals, and these are
all folds.

## §4. The seal and the quarantine (as first claimed)

**Claim.** *The notary's seal preserves the quarantine chain: what was
excluded from the season's reckoning remains checkable against the sealed
volume, for whatever predicate the inspecting officer may later name.*

[Ed. margin: *false, and the revision's Counterexample kills it: two
closed volumes of two entries each — {(a,+5),(b,−5)};{(a,−5),(b,+5)}
against {(c,+7),(d,−7)};{(c,−7),(d,+7)} — both balancing, both netting
nought, identical under every fold fixed at closing, and parted by the
after-the-fact question "was a five-kroner posting made?" The seal binds;
it does not reveal; and you cannot quarantine after the fact what you did
not think to count. The draft believed the corpus's own C3 as first
stated; the season's counterexample is the refutation our 2026 corpus
also had to write. — Ed.*]

## §5. The rungs (as first resolved)

The wear-ladder keeps each cofire in a rung by age and shifts every H
days. The rungs are **not covered by the balance fold**: the draft's
witness is the pair P₁/P₂ of §4 — different interiors, identical balances
— so no balance-summary answers the rungs; the replay is the only
lossless compaction.

[Ed. margin: *true but weak, and the revision sharpens it outright: the
two-entry permutation [cofire, shift] against [shift, cofire] — same
multiset, different rungs — kills every commutative fold of ANY size, any
state, any cleverness. The witness shrank from a fiber pair to two
entries. The corpus's own sharpening note — from fold-relative to
outright — is this same move.* — Ed.]

## §6. The band and the bank

The rung read-out: W ≤ Ŵ < 2W for any arrivals; expected overstatement
2·ln 2 ≈ 1.386. Skavlan's residue bank: every cofire deposits, the
reservoir leaks, the read-out takes back its high end — and her
conjecture, which this draft ENDORSES on her say-so, that the bank will in
time draw the read-out to the true law, closing the band.

At the noughtth rung the as-built deposit is ~9,100 times too small
against the exact condition.

[Ed. margin: *two faults, both the revision's first business. The 9,100 is
the octave slip — a lost factor of two, 2¹⁵ against 2¹⁶ — the erratum
letter of 12 September corrects it to ~18,262 and prints itself. And the
endorsement dies: the credit is never negative, so it can only widen the
upper ratio — the bank can CENTER the band, never close it; Skavlan's
conjecture is refuted by the same hand that had endorsed it, and the
refutation letter drinks to the ghost. The corpus's RQH correction — the
over-claim refuted, the exact deposit derived, the constant re-carried —
is this same double event.* — Ed.]

## §7

Choose your fold, and you have chosen what your boundary cannot see.

*[End of draft. The draft is the school's most instructive artifact: it is
wrong in three places, twice against its own later self, and every fault
is dated, signed, and kept. — Ed.]*
