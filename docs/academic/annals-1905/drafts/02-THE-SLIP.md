# A SLIP, TO BE PASTED INTO EVERY COPY OF THE SECOND MEMOIR

*[Printed slip, Strandsild cannery press, 4 May 1904. Found folded into the
draft, three copies in the trunk. — Ed.]*

The entry set down in the draft's §7,

    {(G:authority, −1), (T:authority, +1), (G:correction, +|g−s|)},

**does not balance.** Its legs sum to −1 + 1 + |g−s| = |g−s|, which is
nought only when nothing needed correcting — that is, only when the entry
is not posted at all. I set the three legs down as the trade's custom sets
a correction, and I did not sum them, and the fault is mine.

The entry that obeys the first rule carries a fourth leg:

    {(G:authority, −1), (T:authority, +1),
     (G:correction, +|g−s|), (T:correction-issued, −|g−s|)},

the correction booked as a paired accrual: debit the expense, credit the
contra. Sums to nought. All theorems of the memoir are stated for the
four-legged form.

Found at the second pass of the proof sheets, 3 May 1904, and against my
own draft, which is where the second pass looks first.

— J. Fosse, second bookkeeper
countersigned: S. Undrum, chief bookkeeper
