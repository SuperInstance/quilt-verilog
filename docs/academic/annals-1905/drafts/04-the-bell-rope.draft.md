# DRAFT — THE BELL-ROPE

*[First draft, M. Holt, December 1904–January 1905. Two faults, both caught
by letter before Kristiania saw the memoir: the clean form quoted without
its one-sided hypothesis (Vik), and the key-moving adversary that
under-delivers on the plane (Vik again, with the repair Grønn demanded be
verified at length). The revised memoir is Paper IV. — Ed.]*

**Marius Holt**

## §1

The audit comes by mail boat, F days stale. The truth drifts at ρ a day.
The court re-sights on the letters. The question: how good can any schedule
be?

## §2. The lemmas (as in the revision)

Two seasons agreeing in every frame to day τ send the same letters to day
τ + F, and no rule can tell them apart. Every anchor is at least F days
old. Drift since the anchor is error.

## §3. The theorem (as first claimed)

**The floor.** *For every rule there is a season on which the rule's error
is at least* **the mass of margins within ρ·F** *— and the adversary is
plain: hold the season quiet until the last letter is written, then move
the key itself across the boundary band at rate ρ through the dark
window.*

[Ed. margin: two faults, printed as found.

*Fault one (the form).* The clean mass-of-margins form holds when the
boundary mass is one-sided — all of it on the answered side. On two-sided
mass the adversary must choose a side for each key, and a key MOVED
crosses one side only; quoting the clean form overclaims by up to half.
The revision's Corollary 1 carries the swept mass and the condition, and
is framed — by Holt himself — as a repair of his own first draft.

*Fault two (the mechanism).* On the plane, a key moved by β flips a lens
of mass growing as β^(3/2) against the band's own β — the move delivers
strictly less than the claim, and the theorem as drafted is not merely
loose but unproven at its own bound. The repair is Vik's: do not move the
key; **redraft the gauge itself outward**, every question-to-key distance
growing by ρ a day — the legality wants the three triangle cases written
out, which Grønn demanded and the revision prints in the minute-book
appendix. With the outward redraft the swept band is attained exactly.

*The corpus's own history, for the reader who wants it: the 2026 sketch
first moved a key; the machine-held paper replaced the move with the
metric-perturbation construction and flagged the sketch's overclaim — the
same two faults, found the same ways, a hundred and twenty years apart. —
Ed.*]

## §4. The schedule arithmetic (as in the revision, and correct as first
written — the draft's arithmetic was Fosse's to check and it held)

T_w = ε₀/ρ − F; equal spacing; the roster split c/T_w against m·c/T_w; the
phase rule; the worked season with its flags.

## §5. What the memoir does not say

It prices ignorance; it does not cure it. A theorem that says NO with
proof is not a lesser theorem than one that says yes.

*[End of draft. Holt's own note on the back, kept: "V. says I moved the
wrong thing. She is right. The gauge moves, not the fish." — Ed.]*
