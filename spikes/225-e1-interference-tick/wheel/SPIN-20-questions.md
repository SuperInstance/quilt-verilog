## Part A — the raw harvest (grouped, unranked)

### A1. Seams — where two results nearly contradict

- **S1. Compensation vs fresh×K.** Blanket mass-compensation erases the K=2 trough (SPIN-11-pd-comp: kcoh5 53.2→37.6) but fresh×K is the causal grammar law (R²=0.891) and the θ>1 gate preserves K-trough signs *exactly* (SPIN-16). The trough exists, compensation can delete it, the gate can spare it. **What is the K=2 trough made of — nf pile-up mass or pulse-overlap lifetime — and is there a θ<1 gate that cures the trough without touching the fresh×K term?**
- **S2. Wall is a rate; gates are binary; O7 moved the wall.** Divergence iff |1−N/pd| outruns decay within the window (a rate) but the gate opens iff nf ≥ 2pd+1 (binary) — and lag compensation just moved the bundle wall from N=4 to N≈7. **Is there one scalar invariant (effective pd or effective echo factor) that predicts where compensation puts the wall — and does the co-fire wall at N≈7 sit at exactly 2·pd+1 for the fabric's dial pd?** If yes, the EXP4-O2b "two different physics" verdict unifies at the compensated limit.
- **S3. Event ceiling but no debt ceiling.** Events cap at 99.85% occupancy while debt climbs unbounded (9202/event and climbing at drift=384) — yet debt hard-caps ≈460k under δ→1 tightening. **What fixes 460k and 99.85%?** Neither number has a mechanism. Is 99.85 the refractory floor's residue (SPIN-1's 1-tick gap), and is 460k a lattice quantization of debt-per-event at δ=1?
- **S4. Cost tracks m_s; performance tracks n_f.** Toll/event spread (29–38%) ranks stably by stale-mass, while residency is caused by fresh-count (drop-m_s costs only 0.018 R²). The same fabric bills one axis and performs on the other. **Do (toll, residency) generate a Pareto frontier on the (m_s, n_f) plane — and is the best grammar at the knee of that frontier, or does a grammar exist that dominates on both axes?**
- **S5. Anti-sync and memory are substitutes; AS and gate are domain-disjoint; yet AS×scheduler stacks superadditively (+35.3/+11.8).** Three pairwise composition verdicts, no ternary map. **Is composition transitive — if A⊥B and B⊥C, is A×C predictable without running it?** (The SPIN-15 charter pre-registers the pairwise law; nobody has asked about transitivity.)
- **S6. Memorylessness vs resonance.** The spread knee is memoryless (loop area ~0, SPIN-16) yet the K=2 regime-switching tax is catastrophic (27pp) and phase-resonant with reality's 240-cycle (SPIN-17). **How can a system be memoryless in level but phase-resonant in rate?** Hypothesis: memory lives in the pulse population (settling ≈131 ticks), not the spread state — so "memoryless" was measured on the wrong state variable.

### A2. Standing unexplained numbers (each is a question)

- **U1. The 0.75–0.78·2Δ knee constant.** Three scalings exist (knee=0.75·2Δ; knee≈1.2–1.25·δ; span-budget (max−min)·σ≲2Δ) with unexplained coefficients. **Do all published knees collapse onto ONE curve when replotted in normalized units (span·σ)/2Δ or knee/δ — and if yes, does the coefficient follow from the decay functional (∫ decay over the lag distribution crossing threshold) rather than fitting?**
- **U2. Expiry evaporation ≈ 0.17–0.30/event.** Booked as a real channel (SPIN-15), never characterized. **Is evaporation/event a constant of the fabric, or a function of (K, pd, grammar)?** If it co-moves with the 0.75-knee constants it is the decay law's tail; if not, it is a second mechanism the ledger books but no one models.
- **U3. Toll spread tracks m_s (29–38%).** Mechanism unknown. **Is toll/event linear in m_s at fixed K (then a two-parameter toll law exists, mirroring the grammar law), or does the K=2 pathology (+9–12 debt/ev) break linearity everywhere?**
- **U4. κ-detector 90-tick lag vs 1600-tick dwell vs τ≈131 pulse settling.** 90 ≈ 131 within a factor. **Is detector lag lower-bounded by pulse lifetime — i.e., is the O4 debt-gate failure a physics limit, not a detector-design failure?** Vary the lifetime dial; if lag tracks lifetime 1:1, no detector redesign helps and debt gates must be predictive (hysteresis-free knees, S6) instead of reactive.
- **U5. The 0.31·δ slope of ρ(δ).** Confirmed on two independent blades, never derived. Same question family as U1: **is 0.31 the decay law's fingerprint?**

### A3. Instrument scars → questions

- **I1. Guard-prefix artifact (SPIN-16):** never-open arms carried a bailout guard → prefix statistics gamed a registered selection rule. **How many earlier sweeps contain flag-blind selection rules?** Concrete: re-scan every published sweep whose arms can diverge for divergence-flag-conditioned selection; book which verdicts survive.
- **I2. Mislabeled-arm canaries caught two bugs (round-2 O2, DEQ-2 cold/hot).** **What is the canary detection rate of the wheel as a process?** Inject N deliberate label swaps into lanes; measure catch rate. If <100%, some published MIXED verdicts are suspect by construction.
- **I3. Chunked re-simulation resets the LCG (SPIN-16 scar).** **Which published numbers came from chunked lanes?** Same audit family, cheaper: grep the lane scripts for chunk boundaries, re-run the affected configs single-pass, diff.

### A4. Negative space — questions by inversion

- **V1. If span-budget (max−min)·σ ≲ 2Δ is false, what must be true instead?** Then every knee is a rounding-lattice artifact of its own spec (SPIN-9's round(i·s/5) mechanism generalized) — knees would be *unportable across substrates*. That would make U1 the most important question on the board (it decides whether any knee number survives a spec change).
- **V2. If the K=2 P=16 catastrophe is a fabric law, it must survive replacing reality.** Every sweep varies fabric knobs against ONE fixed reality trace (80/40/120 harmonics, 240-cycle). **Is the 27pp tax a property of the fabric or of this particular reality?** Nobody has varied reality.
- **V3. If toll/event cannot be made grammar-invariant (NO-GO stands), then a grammar-blind debt gate can never be safe.** **What is the minimal per-grammar gate table, and does it collapse to (m_s, K) — two numbers every operator already knows before deployment?**
- **V4. If fresh×K is causal and MUTE jumps residency 71.5→95.6 (removing a twin HELPS), then scheduling is not the only lever.** **Does mute-the-stalest-twin beat every spread scheduler on the same grammars?** If yes, the optimal controller may be a pruner, not a scheduler.
- **V5. If GC-C1 (six verbs suffice) is true, the θ-gate must reduce to six verbs.** The gate reads nf (concurrent trigger count). **Is nf even expressible as a qm_view readout?** If not, the SPIN-16 gate is the first live seventh-verb candidate — exactly the (𝕂*, ω*, I) triple GC-C1 asks someone to produce.

### A5. Substrate questions (what would the FPGA answer that Python can't?)

- **B1. The README's honest wall: rounds are "model findings, candidate spec limits, listed nowhere as silicon properties."** **Does the spread knee exist in fabric RTL at all?** The Python lane serializes per-tick decay; the RTL sweeps edges in parallel within the tick deadline. If the knee is a serialization artifact, half the beacon table describes a phantom. One verilator run of the SPIN-4 ladder (spreads 0/10/15/20/30) against the same QUF decides it.
- **B2. Python is not a miter (no equivalence proof).** **Is there ANY config where integer-Python and RTL diverge on residency — and is the divergence bounded by ±7 quanta (O3's floor) or unbounded?** A differential sweep on the ladder family turns "no formal equivalence" from a disclaimer into a number.
- **B3. The tick deadline is proven unstarvable under flood (BMC) — but the knee lives at 15 ticks of spread.** **Does the RTL tick deadline bind before the knee does?** I.e., at what spread does tick servicing latency exceed the deadline contract in real cycles? That number is the true fabric ceiling the Python model cannot see.

### A6. Fisherman/boat questions (the captain's √8mm-vs-3 doctrine)

- **F1. The fisherman rigs 3 because it's on the shelf.** The learned scheduler's coefficients are full-precision integers. **Does the +5.7pp gain survive quantizing coefficients (and the θ dial) to ±7 / 4-bit — the O3 shelf?** If yes, the scheduler deploys on ESP32 alongside the fabric; if no, the scheduler needs more precision than the thing it controls — a real deployment boundary.
- **F2. A boat 60 miles offshore trains on day-0 reality and can't retrain.** **What is the model half-life of the learned scheduler under reality drift?** Train v2 on baseline reality, test across drift dial values, find where gain crosses 0. This number is the retraining schedule a boat must actually sail.
- **F3. The fish doesn't care how many digits you had (SPIN-13e doctrine).** **What tolerance does the *fabric itself* demand — do any verified beacon numbers move when dials are snapped to the ±7 lattice?** Same doctrine as F1 applied to the knobs, not the scheduler.
